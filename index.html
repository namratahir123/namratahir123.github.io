Welcome to Urdu with Miss N!

Urdu with Miss N is a learning platform designed to help you learn Urdu step by step, from the basics to everyday conversations. Through easy-to-follow video lessons, you can explore Urdu vocabulary, the alphabet, reading, writing, grammar, and practical conversations.

This website brings all the YouTube lessons together in one place, along with worksheets and practice materials to help you revise and improve your skills.

Whether you're a complete beginner, a non-native Urdu learner, a student, or someone who wants to reconnect with the language, you'll find useful resources to build your confidence and learn at your own pace.

Would you like personalised guidance? You can also visit my Preply profile through the link on this website to book one-to-one Urdu lessons.
